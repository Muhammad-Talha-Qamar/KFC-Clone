Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information we collect

We may collect details you provide when ordering or creating an account — such as name, phone number, email, delivery address, and payment-related information. We also collect basic usage data to improve the site experience.

How we use your information

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Manage accounts, rewards, and customer support
  • Send order updates and (with consent) promotional offers
  • Improve our menu, app, and website performance

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information may be shared with delivery partners, payment processors, and service providers only as needed to fulfill your order or operate the platform.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so please keep your account credentials private.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by reaching out through our Contact Us page. You can also opt out of marketing messages at any time.
